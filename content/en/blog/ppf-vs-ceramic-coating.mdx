Paint protection film stops physical damage such as stone chips; ceramic coating adds gloss and makes cleaning easier. They solve different problems, so many owners use both.

## Paint protection film

PPF is a thick, clear urethane layer. It absorbs impacts from gravel and sand on UAE highways, and light scratches heal with heat.

## Ceramic coating

A ceramic coating is a thin, hard layer that bonds to paint. It repels water and dust but does not stop stone chips.

## Which should you choose?

- Mostly highway driving: start with PPF on the front of the car.
- Mostly city driving and easy maintenance: ceramic coating.
- Best of both: PPF on high-impact areas, ceramic coating over everything.
