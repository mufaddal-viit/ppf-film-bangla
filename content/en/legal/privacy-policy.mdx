This policy explains how PPF Phantom Car Care L.L.C. ("we") collects and uses personal data, in line with the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021).

## What we collect

- **Enquiry details** you send through our forms: name, phone number, optional email, vehicle details, the service you are interested in and your message.
- **Consent record**: that you agreed to be contacted, when, and which version of this policy applied.
- **Attribution data**: the page you first landed on, the referring website and campaign (UTM) parameters, stored in your browser and sent with your enquiry.
- **Analytics data**, only if you accept analytics cookies.

## Why we use it

We use enquiry details only to respond to your request, prepare quotes and provide the services you ask for. We do not sell personal data.

## How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept for up to 24 months after our last contact with you, unless you become a customer and longer retention is required by law.

## Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw your consent, by emailing us. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that happened before.

## Cookies

Essential storage keeps the site working and remembers your cookie choice. Analytics cookies (Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics) are only set after you accept them, and you can change your choice by clearing your browser storage.
